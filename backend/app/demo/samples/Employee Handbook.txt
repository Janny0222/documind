# Welcome to Lumen & Pine

Lumen & Pine is a fictional 40-person design studio. This handbook is sample content for the DocuMind demo; it does not describe any real company.

# Working hours and remote work

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Outside core hours you can plan your own day.
Employees may work remotely up to three days a week. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from your team lead and People Operations.
The studio provides a one-time home-office allowance of 600 USD for equipment, claimable within your first 90 days.

# Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year, accrued monthly. Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year; anything beyond that expires on 31 March.
Request vacation at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days.
Sick leave is separate: 10 paid days per year. A doctor's note is required after three consecutive sick days.
Parental leave is 16 weeks at full pay for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks for the secondary caregiver.

# Expenses

Submit expense claims within 30 days of the purchase, with a receipt for anything above 25 USD.
Client meals are reimbursed up to 60 USD per person. Alcohol is not reimbursable.
Economy class is the default for flights under six hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights.

# Learning budget

Every employee has a yearly learning budget of 1,000 USD for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over.

# Leaving the studio

Employees give 30 days written notice before resigning. Managers and team leads give 60 days.
During the notice period you are expected to hand over active projects and document open work.
Company equipment must be returned within 7 days of your last working day.

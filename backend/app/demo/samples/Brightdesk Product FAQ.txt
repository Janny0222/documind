# Brightdesk product FAQ

Brightdesk is a fictional help-desk app made up for the DocuMind demo.

# Plans and pricing

Brightdesk has three plans. Starter is free for up to 3 agents. Team costs 15 USD per agent per month. Business costs 39 USD per agent per month and adds single sign-on and audit logs.
Annual billing gives a 20% discount on Team and Business.

# Trial and cancellation

Every paid plan starts with a 14-day free trial; no credit card is needed.
You can cancel at any time from Settings > Billing. Monthly plans stop at the end of the current billing period. Annual plans are refunded pro rata within the first 30 days only.

# Data and security

Customer data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt) by default. US hosting is available on the Business plan.
Backups run every 6 hours and are kept for 30 days.
Brightdesk encrypts data in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256.

# Support

Starter customers get community support. Team customers get email support with a response within one business day. Business customers get 24/7 chat support with a 1-hour response target for urgent tickets.

# Integrations

Brightdesk connects to Slack, Microsoft Teams, Jira and Shopify. A public REST API is available on Team and Business plans, limited to 600 requests per minute.

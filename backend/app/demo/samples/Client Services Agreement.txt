# Client Services Agreement (sample)

This agreement is between Lumen & Pine Design Co. ("the Studio") and Harbor Light Coffee ("the Client"). Both parties are fictional; this is sample content for the DocuMind demo.

# Scope of work

The Studio will deliver a brand identity (logo, color palette, typography guidelines) and a five-page marketing website.
Each deliverable includes two rounds of revisions. Additional revisions are billed at the hourly rate of 95 USD.

# Fees and payment terms

The total project fee is 18,000 USD.
40% is due when the agreement is signed, 40% on approval of the brand identity, and 20% on website launch.
Invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date (net 30).
Late payments incur a fee of 1.5% per month on the overdue amount.
The Studio may pause work if any invoice is more than 45 days overdue.

# Timeline

The project starts on the signing date and is planned to take 12 weeks.
Delays caused by late Client feedback extend the timeline by the same number of days.

# Intellectual property

Ownership of final deliverables transfers to the Client after full payment.
The Studio may show the work in its portfolio unless the Client objects in writing.

# Confidentiality

Both parties keep the other's confidential information private for three years after the agreement ends.

# Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice.
If the Client terminates, it pays for all work completed up to the termination date.
Either party may terminate immediately if the other materially breaches the agreement and does not fix it within 14 days of notice.

# Liability

The Studio's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim.
